# WeGo Cooperative — Membership Agreement Summary
### Plain-English Version for Prospective Members

> **This is not the legal agreement.** This is a human explanation of what you are agreeing to when you become a WeGo founding member. The full legal membership agreement governs in all cases. If anything in this summary conflicts with the legal agreement, the legal agreement controls.

---

## 1. What You Are Joining

WeGo is a **worker-owned cooperative**. When you sign the membership agreement, you are not becoming an employee of a corporation — you are becoming a **part-owner of the company itself**.

Your $500 membership fee is not a deposit, a subscription, or a fee to use an app. It is your **cooperative share** — your entry into an ownership stake in WeGo.

Every WeGo member has:
- **One vote** in all cooperative elections (one member, one vote — not weighted by income or seniority)
- The right to run for the Member-Elected Board of Trustees
- Access to annual financial reports and pension fund statements
- A share in cooperative surplus if distributed

## 2. What You Pay

### $500 One-Time Membership Fee
Payable over 12 months at $41.67/month. This is your cooperative share. It is not refundable in Year 1 if you voluntarily leave.

### $75/Month Membership Dues
Continues as long as you are an active member. Breakdown of where it goes:

| Allocation | Monthly Amount | Purpose |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| Insurance pool | ~$20 | Contribution to group commercial policy |
| Retirement Trust | $25 | Seeds your pension directly |
| Operations | ~$30 | App infrastructure, payment processing, admin |

Dues are adjusted annually by the Consumer Price Index (CPI) to protect the retirement trust from inflation erosion.

### 12% Platform Fee on Every Fare
WeGo retains 12% of every fare. You keep **88%**. The 12% is allocated as follows:

| Allocation | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| ~5% | Your retirement contribution (SEP-IRA or pension pool) |
| ~4% | Insurance top-up (group policy cost above your $20 dues) |
| ~3% | Technology, payment processing, cooperative reserves, hardware fund |

The 12% cap is written into the cooperative's founding constitution and requires a **75% supermajority member vote** to change. No board or executive can raise it unilaterally.

## 4. Your Retirement — Two Tracks

WeGo contributes **5% of your annual earnings** to your retirement every month from the 12% platform fee. Which track you are on depends on your age at enrollment.

### Track 1 — Under 50: SEP-IRA (Personal Account)

WeGo deposits 5% of your earnings into a **SEP-IRA in your name** at Vanguard or Fidelity every month.

- You **own** this account — it stays with you if you ever leave WeGo
- Grows with the market, tax-deferred
- Withdrawals before age 59½ incur a 10% IRS penalty plus income tax
- Withdrawals after 59½ taxed as ordinary income (no penalty)
- You can additionally open a **Roth IRA** yourself (up to $7,000/year) on top of WeGo's contribution

**30-Year Projection (driver earning $80,000/year in California, married):**

| | Amount |
|-|--------|
| WeGo contributes to SEP-IRA | $4,000/year |
| Driver contributes to Roth IRA | $7,000/year |
| Total invested per year | $11,000 |
| Total contributed over 30 years | $330,000 |
| Projected balance at 7% return | ~$1,040,000 (nominal) |
| In today's purchasing power (inflation-adjusted) | ~$427,000 |
| Monthly withdrawal (4% rule, today's dollars) | ~$1,426/month |

### Track 2 — Age 50 and Over: Defined Benefit Pension Pool

WeGo's 5% contribution goes into the **cooperative pension pool**. WeGo guarantees a fixed monthly payout for life based on years of service — not market performance.

| Years Driven with WeGo | Monthly Payout for Life |
|-----------------------|------------------------|
| 5 years | ~$750/month |
| 8 years | ~$1,200/month |
| 10 years | ~$1,500/month |
| 15 years | ~$2,250/month |

Payout begins the month you stop driving. No market risk. No waiting for compound interest.

## 5. The 20-Year Legacy Pension

Every member who completes **20 years of active membership** receives the Driver's Legacy Pension — regardless of which retirement track they are on.

### At 20 Years, You Receive Three Things Simultaneously:

**1. $20,000 Equity Payout**
Your accumulated internal capital account, paid as a lump sum on the day you complete 20 years.

**2. ~$2,500/Month Pension for Life**
Begins immediately. Funded from the Retirement Trust (member contributions + investment returns + AV fleet income). Designed to track inflation — not a fixed nominal amount.

**3. Zero Platform Fee Forever**
After Year 20, the 12% platform fee drops to $0 permanently. You keep 100% of every fare for as long as you choose to keep driving — alongside your monthly pension.

### Vesting Schedule (Partial Pension If You Leave Early)

| 3–5 years | 25% vested |
| 5–10 years | 50% vested |

### Pension Transfer to Family

## 8. What Happens If You Leave

### Voluntarily
- Pension vesting is frozen at your current level (0%–90% depending on years served)
- Your SEP-IRA stays with you — it is in your name at Vanguard or Fidelity
- Your $500 membership share may be redeemable per cooperative bylaws
- You lose access to the group insurance policy and platform immediately

### Involuntarily (Expelled by the Cooperative)
- Expulsion requires a formal process and a member board vote — not a management decision
- You retain your SEP-IRA balance
- Pension vesting is subject to the expulsion ruling terms

### Death
- SEP-IRA passes to your designated beneficiary (set directly with Vanguard/Fidelity)
- Pension transferability follows your service tier (see Section 5 above)

## 9. What Happens If WeGo Fails

### Your SEP-IRA Is Fully Protected
It is held in your name at Vanguard or Fidelity — entirely separate from WeGo's accounts. If WeGo closes tomorrow, your SEP-IRA is completely unaffected.

### The Pension Pool Has Three Layers of Protection

**Layer 1 — ERISA Qualified Trust**
The Retirement Trust is a separate legal entity. WeGo's management cannot access, borrow from, or pledge it. Creditors of the cooperative cannot reach it.

**Layer 2 — Joint Board of Trustees**
The trust is governed by a board that is 50% elected driver-members and 50% independent fiduciaries. These fiduciaries have a legal duty only to pension beneficiaries — not to cooperative management. Their appointments survive any change in leadership.

**Layer 3 — Constitutional Supermajority Lock**
The ERISA separation of the pension fund cannot be removed during the cooperative's first 10 years, and requires a 75% supermajority member vote after that. A simple board majority cannot vote away your pension protections.

## 10. Your Voting Rights

Every member has exactly **one vote** — regardless of how much they drive, how long they have been a member, or how much they have paid in. Voting power cannot be purchased or accumulated.

Members vote on:
- Election of the Member Board of Trustees
- Changes to the cooperative's founding constitution
- Distribution of annual surplus
- Any amendment to the 12% platform fee cap or pension structure

## 11. Your Obligations as a Member

By signing the WeGo Membership Agreement you agree to:

1. Pay the $500 membership fee (over 12 months at $41.67/month)
2. Pay $75/month dues while actively driving
3. Maintain your vehicle to WeGo's Member Standards (cleanliness, safety, age requirements)
4. Follow the cooperative's Driver Code of Conduct
5. Accept the cooperative's formal dispute resolution process before seeking outside legal action
6. Drive exclusively through the WeGo platform (no simultaneous active Uber/Lyft accounts while on duty)

---

## 12. WeGo's Obligations to You

In return, WeGo is contractually bound to:

1. Never charge more than 12% platform fee
2. Contribute 5% of your earnings to your retirement account every month
3. Provide group commercial insurance from your first active day
4. Fund and protect the pension trust under ERISA
5. Give you one vote in all member elections, with equal weight to every other member
6. Publish full annual financial transparency reports
7. Pay your $20,000 equity payout and full pension at 20 years of service
8. Drop your platform fee to 0% permanently after 20 years
