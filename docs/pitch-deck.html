Your Protections

Your Money Is Locked Away
From Management. By Law.

ERISA Qualified Trust

The pension fund is a separate legal entity. Management cannot touch it. Creditors cannot reach it. Same legal protection as major U.S. pension funds.

Joint Board of Trustees

50% elected drivers. 50% independent fiduciaries. Their legal duty is only to you — not to cooperative management. Their appointments survive leadership changes.

Supermajority Lock

Pension protections cannot be removed for 10 years. After that, 75% of members must vote to change them. No executive or board majority can take your pension away.

Your SEP-IRA is in your name at Vanguard or Fidelity. If WeGo closes tomorrow, your retirement account is completely unaffected.
