Accessibility Statement

WeGo is committed to making our website and platform accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. This page describes our current accessibility efforts and how to report issues.

Our Commitment

WeGo believes transportation should be available to everyone. We are working to ensure our website meets WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards and that the WeGo app meets the accessibility requirements of California ADA and CPUC regulations for transportation network companies.

Current Website Accessibility Features

  • All navigation is keyboard-accessible
  • Color contrast meets WCAG AA minimums in both light and dark mode
  • All images include descriptive alt attributes
  • Form fields include visible labels
  • FAQ accordion buttons use aria-expanded to communicate state to screen readers
  • Interactive elements have focus-visible outlines
  • Page titles are descriptive and unique per page
  • Dark mode toggle available for users with light sensitivity

Known Limitations

This website is under active development. Some areas may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. We are actively working to address all known gaps before the public app launch. If you encounter a barrier, please contact us using the information below — we prioritize accessibility feedback.

App Accessibility (Coming at Launch)

The WeGo app will include:

  • Full VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android) support
  • Large text and high-contrast mode support
  • Assisted mobility service category for riders who need wheelchair-accessible vehicles
  • In-app text relay option for deaf or hard-of-hearing passengers

Assisted Mobility Service

WeGo's Assisted Mobility service tier provides wheelchair-accessible vehicles (WAV) operated by drivers trained in mobility assistance. This service is available for advance booking via our contact form until the app launches. There is no premium charge — the same cooperative fare structure applies.

Report an Accessibility Issue

If you find a part of this website or the WeGo app difficult to use due to a disability, please tell us:

  • Email: accessibility@wegocoop.com
  • Contact form: wegocoop.com/contact (select "General Inquiry")

We will respond within 2 business days and work with you to provide the information or service in an accessible format.
