1. About WeGo

WeGo Cooperative ("WeGo," "we," "us") is a California worker cooperative organized under the California Cooperative Corporation Law. We operate a technology platform connecting driver-members with passengers in the San Francisco Bay Area.

2. Cooperative Membership

Driver membership in WeGo is governed by the Cooperative Member Agreement, which is provided separately and requires individual signature. Membership confers ownership rights, voting rights, and obligations as described in the Member Agreement and cooperative bylaws.

3. Platform Use

Use of the WeGo app — whether as a driver-member or as a passenger — is subject to applicable California law, CPUC Transportation Network Company regulations, and WeGo's operating policies. By using the platform, you agree to comply with all applicable rules and the code of conduct described in your member or rider agreement.

4. Fees and Earnings

The WeGo fee structure (12% platform fee, 88% driver retention) is described in detail in the Member Agreement and on our Features page. Fee terms may be updated by a majority cooperative member vote. Members will be notified of any changes in advance per the bylaws.

5. Retirement & Benefits

Retirement contributions, pension pool participation, and benefit eligibility are governed by the Member Agreement and separate benefit plan documents. Projected figures on this website are estimates, not guarantees. See our FAQ for details.

6. Limitation of Liability

WeGo's liability to any member or user is limited as described in the full Member Agreement. Membership offerings described on this website are not securities under applicable federal and California law.

7. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of California. Disputes are subject to the dispute resolution process described in the Member Agreement, including mediation before any litigation.
