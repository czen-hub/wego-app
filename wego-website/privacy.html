Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026. This policy describes what data WeGo collects, how we use it, and your rights under California law (CCPA/CPRA).

Note: WeGo is a pre-launch cooperative. Our full Privacy Policy is being finalized by legal counsel and will be published here before the Bay Area app launch. The sections below describe our current data practices for this website and membership application process.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an application or contact form on this website, we collect the information you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, and vehicle information. We do not sell this information to any third party.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to: (a) respond to your inquiry or application; (b) send you updates about WeGo's launch and cooperative membership; (c) process your membership application and payments; and (d) comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Data Sharing

WeGo does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with service providers who assist us in operating the platform (e.g., payment processors, background check providers) under strict confidentiality agreements. We will disclose data if required by law.

4. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use minimal cookies for theme preferences (stored locally) and analytics to understand how visitors use the site. No advertising tracking cookies are used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to: (a) know what personal information we collect; (b) request deletion of your data; (c) opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data); and (d) non-discrimination for exercising these rights. To make a request, email privacy@wegocoop.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain membership application data for the duration of your membership relationship with WeGo and as required by law. You may request deletion of your pre-membership application data at any time.

7. Security

WeGo uses industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) and secure cloud storage. No system is perfectly secure; we encourage you to use strong passwords and notify us of any suspected breach.

8. Contact

Privacy questions? Email privacy@wegocoop.com or use our Contact page.
